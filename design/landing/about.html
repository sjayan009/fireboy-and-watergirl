About

About this rebuild

Fireboy & Watergirl: The Forest Temple, online co-op is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project by Jayan Sirikonda. It takes the 2009 Flash game and makes it playable online with a friend, for the first time, in any modern browser.

What it is

The whole Forest Temple: 32 levels, two characters, one of them afraid of water and the other of fire. Two people can play side by side on one keyboard with no account, or sign in and play together from anywhere. Progress belongs to the pair, so whichever of you invites next, you resume from the same level. Friends can watch a game live if you let them, and the leaderboard keeps the fastest pairs on each level.

How it was made

There was no source code: one compiled Flash file, 1,855,972 bytes. The levels, the physics, the art and the audio were all recovered from it, the game was ported to TypeScript, and it was given rollback netcode so two people in two browsers play as if they shared a keyboard. For Developers tells that story in detail, with the numbers.

Who runs it

Jayan Sirikonda built it and runs it. It is free, sells nothing, shows no ads and runs no analytics. The website is hosted on Cloudflare and accounts are run on Supabase; the Privacy Policy lists everything that is stored and who processes it.

The original game

Fireboy & Watergirl was created by Oslo Albet. The Forest Temple came out in 2009, and its art and audio are his. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oslo Albet.
