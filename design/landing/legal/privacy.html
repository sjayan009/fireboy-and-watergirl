Privacy Policy

This site is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project run by Jayan Sirikonda. It collects only what online play needs. It sells nothing, shows no ads, and runs no analytics or tracking cookies.

Playing without an account

Playing as a guest, two players on one keyboard, needs no account. Your progress stays in your browser's local storage and is not sent to us, unless you later sign in and choose to add it to your account.

What an account stores

  • Email address and password, to sign you in. The password is stored only as a hash. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your Google email address and basic profile details from Google.
  • Username, which other players use to find you.
  • Friends: who you have added, invited or blocked.
  • Progress: levels completed, medals and best times, for solo play and for each pair of friends.
  • Leaderboard times, shown to other signed-in players unless you turn on "Keep my times off the leaderboard" in Settings.
  • Two-factor settings, if you turn them on, and hashed recovery codes.
  • Live game sessions: while you play online, your inputs travel to your friend directly or through a relay. Watchers see a game only if you let them. Live play itself is not recorded.
  • Ranked replays: when a pair finishes a level for the leaderboard, the game uploads that run's inputs so the time can be checked by replaying it.

We also keep limited counters, such as how many friend requests or username checks an account has made, to stop abuse.

Network data

Like any website, the services below see your IP address when your browser connects to them. Online play uses WebRTC, which can share your IP address with the friend you are playing with so the two of you can connect directly. If a direct connection is not possible, the game routes through a relay instead.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, relays game traffic when a direct connection is not possible, and runs Turnstile, the check on the sign-up, sign-in and password-reset forms that keeps bots out. Turnstile looks at signals from your browser to tell people from bots; it does not set advertising cookies.
  • Supabase runs the accounts, the database and the real-time connections.
  • Google, if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Brevo sends sign-up, sign-in and password-reset emails.

They process data only to provide these services. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Sign-up asks for your date of birth first; only whether you passed is kept in your browser, never the date itself. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it. Anyone, of any age, can play locally without an account.

Keeping and deleting it

Your data is kept until you delete your account. Settings → Delete This Account removes your profile, friendships and progress immediately. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove this game's access from your Google account. You can also ask us by email to access, correct, export or delete your data, and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Who can read what (your own data, a friend's shared progress, leaderboard times) is enforced by the database itself. Two-factor sign-in is available.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. For anything else, contact Jayan Sirikonda at sirikonda.jayan9@gmail.com.
