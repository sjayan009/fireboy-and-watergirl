What to write about

  • Bugs: a level that does not play the way it did in 2009, an online game that will not connect, or anything else that looks wrong. Say which browser you used, which level, and what happened.
  • Your account: trouble signing in, a username, or two-factor sign-in.
  • Your data: a request to access, correct, export or delete it. These are answered within 30 days, as the Privacy Policy says.
  • A child under 13 with an account. Tell us and it will be deleted.
  • Rights: if you hold rights in the original game and have a concern about this project.

Before you write

Some things you can do straight away. Settings → Delete This Account removes your profile, friendships and progress immediately, and the sign-in form can send you a password reset. Never send a password or a recovery code by email: nobody here will ever ask for one.
