Fireboy & Watergirl: The Forest Temple, online co-op

The 2009 Flash game Fireboy & Watergirl in the Forest Temple, now online multiplayer for the first time. Play side by side on one keyboard with no account, or invite a friend and play together from anywhere. It is free, runs in the browser, and has no ads. Rebuilt for online co-op by Jayan Sirikonda.

Play locally right now · Sign in to play online

Online multiplayer, from sign-in to level one

  1. Sign in with Google, or with an email address you confirm. Two-factor sign-in is there if you want it.
  2. Invite a friend and choose who plays Fireboy and who plays Watergirl.
  3. Play, and come back together: progress belongs to the pair, so you resume from the same level.

Online play uses rollback netcode over WebRTC, so two people in two browsers play as if they shared a keyboard.

Watch and compete

Watch a friend's game live, if they let you, or chase the fastest pairs on each level's leaderboard.

The game

All 32 levels of the Forest Temple, recovered from the original compiled Flash file with no source code and ported to TypeScript. This site needs JavaScript to play.
